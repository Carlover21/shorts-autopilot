Shorts Autopilot

Shorts Autopilot is a personal desktop tool that produces short history videos and posts them, on a schedule, to the creator's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

How it works

  1. The creator signs in with TikTok once and approves the app.
  2. The tool writes a script, records a voiceover, adds stock footage and captions.
  3. The finished video is uploaded to the creator's own account using TikTok's Content Posting API.

The tool only posts to the account that authorised it. It does not read, collect or post to anyone else's account.
