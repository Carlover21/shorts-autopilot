Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Shorts Autopilot ("the app") is a personal desktop tool. It runs only on its owner's computer and has no servers, user accounts or databases of its own.

What the app accesses

  • TikTok: your basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to post videos to your account (user.info.basic, video.publish).
  • YouTube and Instagram: permission to upload videos to your channel or account, and the view counts of videos the app posted.

How it is used

This access is used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's accounts and to see how those videos perform. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file. They are never sent anywhere except back to the platform that issued them.

Deleting your data

Revoke the app's access in your TikTok settings under Security → Manage app permissions (or the equivalent setting on YouTube and Instagram). The locally stored token then stops working, and deleting the app's folder removes it completely.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
